Autism Spectrum Disorder (ASD)

Differently abled

Autism Spectrum Disorder (ASD) is a neurodevelopmental Disorder affecting how individuals communicate, interact, and experience the world.

It is a spectrum with widely varying strengths and support needs, characterized by unique social interaction, nonverbal communication, sensory processing,
and intense interests.
